About Us

Find out what makes our salon glow!

Located in Godley, Texas, TanDat Wildcat is a client-focused salon with a variety of beauty and wellness services to meet your every need! We strive to provide a luxurious and high-quality experience for each individual that walks through our doors. We're a family here at TanDat Wildcat, and we want our salon to feel like a home-away-from home for you.

Not only do we provide tanning services such as UV beds and spray tans, TanDat Wildcat has expanded the team to provide nail services, red light therapy, lash services, permanent jewelry, and more!
