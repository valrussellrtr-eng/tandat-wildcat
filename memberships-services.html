Memberships & Services

We offer a variety of tanning experiences to suit every level and skin type, thanks to our diverse selection of high-quality tanning beds. Additionally, we offer several other services you can see below!

UV TANNING

Tanning Beds

ProSun Base Bed

Perfect for beginners or those looking to gradually build color, the ProSun Base Bed activates your skin’s existing melanin to create a strong foundation. This bed is included in all of our tanning packages.

Luxura X3

The Luxura X3 is a well-rounded tanning bed designed to deepen your base color while delivering an even, all-over bronzing effect. This bed is available exclusively in our Purple and Gold packages.

Stand-Up Beds

Our stand-up beds provide a unique open-air tanning experience with an open top and full-body exposure, allowing for better airflow and a refreshing breeze. These beds offer both base and bronzing benefits but are among our most powerful options—ideal for experienced tanners. Included in the Purple and Gold packages.

Luxura X7

The Luxura X7 is our most advanced tanning bed, equipped with strategically placed high-intensity lamps for maximum tanning performance. It delivers fast, deep results with a rich, dark bronze finish. This premium bed is available exclusively in our Gold package.

CUSTOM GLOW

Rapid Spray Tan

Our rapid spray tans are personalized airbrush tans applied by hand for an even, streak-free finish. You can customize your color to achieve your desired glow, with a rapid-developing formula that lets you rinse sooner while still getting a beautiful, bronzed look!
